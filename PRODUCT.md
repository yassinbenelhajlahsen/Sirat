# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

ios

## Users
Muslims who want one companion for daily practice: prayer times, Quran, habits and prayer tracking. The widgets are looked at in passing, on the home screen or lock screen, mostly to see the next prayer and how long until it.

## Product Purpose
Sirat is an Islamic companion app (Expo / React Native). The iOS widgets (Next Prayer, Prayer Times, Verse) put the next prayer, the day's times and a pinned Quran verse on the home and lock screens without opening the app.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Widget layouts ship over the air: only `@expo/ui/swift-ui` components and modifiers (shapes, gradients, rotation and strokes included). No custom fonts, bundled images, new widget kinds or families without a new native build.
- Lock screen widgets render without color.
- Three app themes (Default green, Dark navy, Light cream) with a shared gold accent.
- Spelling follows the app (Dhuhr), 12-hour times, Hijri date without an era suffix, Sunrise never in the prayer list.

## Brand Commitments
Warm, calm, gold on deep green. Copy stays simple, no em dashes.

## Product Principles
- The next prayer and its countdown are read in a glance; everything else supports that.
- Islamic character comes from drawn geometry and Arabic lettering, not from glyph decoration or stock symbols.
- Respectful: no figurative imagery.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Arabic and English both legible; sufficient contrast on all three themes.
