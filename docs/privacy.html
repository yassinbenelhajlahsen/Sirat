Privacy Policy

Sirat respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how your information is handled when you use the app.

1. Location Data

Sirat uses your location only to calculate accurate prayer times and display nearby mosques. Location data is never stored, shared, or transmitted to any third-party server.

2. Device Information

Sirat may access basic device data (such as timezone and language settings) to personalize your experience. This data is processed locally on your device and not uploaded anywhere.

3. Prayer Notifications

Notification preferences are stored securely on your device using local storage. Sirat does not track or collect any information about your notification usage.

4. Internet Access

Sirat connects to trusted APIs (such as Aladhan and Google Places) to fetch accurate prayer times and mosque data. These requests are anonymous and do not include personal identifiers.

5. Data Retention

Sirat does not store any personal data on remote servers. All preferences and cached prayer times are stored locally and can be deleted by uninstalling the app.

6. Third-Party Services

Sirat may use APIs from Google Maps, Aladhan, and Expo for functionality. These services have their own privacy policies, which you can review on their websites.

7. Your Consent

By using Sirat, you consent to this privacy policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the app.

8. Contact

For questions or privacy concerns, contact: support@siratapp.com
