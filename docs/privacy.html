Privacy Policy

How Sirat handles your data — simple and privacy-first.

1. Location Data

Sirat uses your location only to calculate accurate prayer times and show nearby mosques. Location data is processed on-device and not stored or shared without your consent.

2. Device Information

Basic device info (timezone, language) may be used to personalize the app. This is processed locally and not uploaded.

3. Prayer Notifications

Notification preferences are saved locally on your device. Sirat does not collect usage telemetry related to notifications.

4. Internet Access

Sirat fetches data from trusted APIs (Aladhan, Google Places) where needed. Requests are anonymous and do not include personal identifiers from the app.

5. Data Retention

No personal data is stored on remote servers by Sirat. Cached preferences and prayer times remain on your device and can be cleared by uninstalling the app.

6. Third-Party Services

Third-party APIs (Google Maps, Aladhan, Expo) are used; review their privacy policies for details.

7. Your Consent

Using Sirat indicates acceptance of this policy. If you disagree, discontinue use of the app.
