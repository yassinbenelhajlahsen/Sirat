Your privacy

Your email is optional. If you're signed in, your account email may be filled in for you. You can clear it before sending.
You can stay anonymous. Reports sent while signed in are linked to your account. To send one with no account or email attached, sign out first and leave the email blank.
Feedback is simple. It sends your message and the app version.
Bug reports add details so I can find the problem. That means your device model, iOS version, time zone, language, prayer method, whether location and notifications are on, and a short log of recent errors, failed requests and screens you visited.
Your location is never included, and nothing is sent until you tap Send.
